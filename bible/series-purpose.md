# Series purpose

Status: purpose locked from the McAulay Family Code. Brand/title still open.

## Working identity

- Working title: Cove and Mars
- Creator: Alex (McAulay)
- Initial audience: two brothers — Cove (older) and the younger brother, called Eden and Mars on the page
- Home: invented (details later). No pets.
- Possible later audience: a broader published children’s series
- Form: illustrated picture books, designed spread-by-spread
- Governing code: `bible/family-code.md`

## Why this series exists

This series exists to help raise strong, confident and kind boys who will one day be capable of leading, contributing and taking responsibility for others.

After a reread, Cove and Eden/Mars (and the children listening) should feel more capable, more connected, and more sure they are loved whether they succeed or fail — not scolded into virtue.

A parent reading aloud should feel they have gone on an adventure, not delivered a sermon.

What must remain true if the cast grows: the definition of strength, the confidence progression, child agency, and the Family Code.

## What “professional children’s publishing quality” means here

- a story a child asks for again
- characters who stay themselves
- adventure with genuine difficulty
- humour and warmth
- a lesson that is lived, not announced
- pictures that tell part of the story
- continuity that would survive a copy-editor
- consistency with the Family Code without sounding like a workbook

## What this series is not

- generic AI bedtime slurry
- a values workbook wearing a plot
- a showcase for adult cleverness
- a story that defines strength as dominance, hardness, popularity, or always winning
- empty “believe in yourself” success
