## Read-aloud

[CREATOR INPUT REQUIRED]

Until specified, default to:

- sentences a parent can speak without stumbling
- varied rhythm rather than one chant for the whole book
- dialogue that is easy to perform in two child voices

## Narration

[CREATOR INPUT REQUIRED]

Open questions:

- Close third on one child, or a warm omniscient?
- How lyrical vs how plain?
- How much repetition as music?

## Names and ages

- The younger brother may be **Eden** or **Mars** on the page. He prefers Eden and also likes Mars. Both are correct.
- Do **not** state the boys’ ages in narration or dialogue unless the brief is specifically a birthday book. Canon remains Cove 5, Eden/Mars 3.
