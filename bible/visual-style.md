## Composition

Default picture-book needs:

- a clear focal point
- a text-safe region
- room for reread details without clutter
- continuity of left/right, time of day, weather, and held objects

## Humour and visual story

Pictures should sometimes:

- show a side action the text ignores
- hide a clue
- echo the children’s problem at a smaller scale
- register an emotion the narrator does not name

Do not illustrate the sentence word-for-word.
