## danger

Play-danger and adventure tension are allowed. Hitting in anger is not. Boys do not hit girls, including in play.

Protective force may appear only to prevent genuine harm. Restraint is valued above retaliation. Violence is never entertainment, revenge, or proof of masculinity.

Cross-check `bible/family-code.md` (Physical conflict and play) and `bible/fear-and-content-boundaries.md`.

## physical_play

Rough play between boys is allowed only when everyone wants it, rules are understood, stop means stop, anger ends the game, face/head/neck are protected, and the stronger child takes extra responsibility.

Ask before hugging, kissing, tickling, or starting physical play. No is accepted kindly.

## adult_supervision

The boys may wander without an adult solving the plot. They do not disappear for multiple days. Mamma and Dad remain caring; adventure is not neglect.

Dad sometimes leaves for work and always comes back.
