# Fear and content boundaries

Status: scaffold. Do not invent extreme content. Leave the dials for Alex.

When a brief would exceed a filled boundary, escalate. When a boundary is still a placeholder, stay conservative: tension is allowed, horror is not, and safety returns on the page.

## age_range

Canon ages: Cove 5, Mars/Eden 3. Do not print those ages in stories unless a brief is about a birthday. Write to that band.

## acceptable_danger

[CREATOR INPUT REQUIRED]

Obstacles, weather, getting lost briefly, things going wrong. No hitting in anger. Boys do not hit girls, including in play. Protective force only to prevent genuine harm. No gore. Adventure tension is not the same as intentionally frightening someone for fun.

## acceptable_suspense

[CREATOR INPUT REQUIRED]

Starter conservative default: page-turn worry and storms are allowed; lingering dread is not.

## separation_from_parents

Boys may wander for an adventure. Not for multiple days. Dad may be away for work; he always returns in the story-world (do not leave him missing at the end of a book). Brief same-book worry is allowed; implied abandonment is not.

## injury

[CREATOR INPUT REQUIRED]

Starter conservative default: scrapes and scares, no graphic injury.

## death

[CREATOR INPUT REQUIRED]

Starter conservative default: no character death. There are no household pets to kill. Do not introduce a pet death.

## villains

[CREATOR INPUT REQUIRED]

If antagonists appear, they need a comprehensible want. Cartoon evil is not the default.

## weapons

[CREATOR INPUT REQUIRED]

Starter conservative default: no real guns; story tools (ropes, lanterns, oars) are fine.

## frightening_imagery

[CREATOR INPUT REQUIRED]

Pictures may be dramatic. They should not give a child an image they cannot shake at bedtime unless Alex raises that ceiling.

## never
