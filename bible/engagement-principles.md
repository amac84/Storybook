# Engagement principles

Status: starting set. Improve this file as real children react to real books.
Do not force every principle into every book. Variety matters. Compiled into each book’s packet as `engagement_notes`.

## What children should frequently experience

- **Anticipation** — a coming thing they can feel.
- **Participation** — a word, gesture, search, or prediction they can join.
- **Prediction** — a pattern they can guess, then a variation.
- **Discovery** — a new fact, place, or meaning that feels found, not handed over.
- **Surprise** — something they did not predict, that still fits.
- **Competence** — a child character doing something that matters.
- **Humour** — delight, not mockery of the child listener.
- **Emotional safety** — tension is real; the container is safe.
- **Tension followed by relief** — the tightness lets go on the page, not in an afterword.
- **Setup/payoff** — an early detail returns changed.

## What stories should often include

- strong page turns
- repeated patterns with variation
- opportunities to notice something before a character does
- background jokes
- visual Easter eggs
- details discovered on rereading
- recurring rituals, phrases, objects, or behaviours
- moments of wonder
- moments where a problem genuinely becomes more difficult
- an emotionally satisfying resolution

## Design-principle tests

Full table: `bible/story-design-principles.md`.

Also ask:

1. Why will a child care what happens?
2. What question pulls them to the next spread?
3. What becomes progressively more difficult?
4. Where can they participate or predict — and feel “I knew that”?
5. What will they only notice on a second read?
6. What phrase might they still say next month?
7. Would this remain a story if the lesson label were peeled off?

## Anti-patterns

See also `bible/forbidden-patterns.md`.

- A first spread that only introduces names and weather
- A middle that tours incidents without worsening
- A climax an adult or narrator explains
- A joke that needs a grown-up knowledge the child does not have, unless the picture also works for the child
